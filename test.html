* Understand Your Body

This assessment provides a general estimate based on the information you enter.

It is designed to help you learn more about your body and lifestyle.


BMI is a general measurement calculated using your height and weight. It can provide a basic overview, but it does not tell the complete story about a person's health.

Recommended calorie intake is an estimate of the energy your body may need each day. Your actual needs can vary depending on factors such as age, activity level, growth, and lifestyle.








Note: These results are estimates intended for educational purposes and should not be treated as medical advice.
