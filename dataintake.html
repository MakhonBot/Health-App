Let's calculate your BMI.

What is BMI?
* BMI or Body Mass Index is a simple tool that uses your height and weight (in centimeters and kilograms, respectively) to estimate if your body weight is healthy.
*It divides your weight in kg by your height in meters squared.

Important limits of BMI:

  • No muscle distinction: it cannot tell the difference between heavy muscle and body fat.
  • No fat placement: It does not show where the fat is in your body.
  • General guide only:It is a fast screening tool; not a full check on personal health.

BMI categories:

  • Underweight: A score below 18.5
  • Normal weight: A score from 18.5 to 24.9
  • Overweight: A score from 25 to 29.9
  • Obese:A score of 30 or higher
