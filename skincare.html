Taking care of your skin is the first step to enjoy a healthy life.
* Let's find your skincare based on your type.💭



* What are skin types?

  • Normal skin: Well-balanced, clear, smooth, and not too oily or dry.
  • Oily skin: Produces excess sebum, leading to a glossy shine, enlarged pores, and acne.
  • Dry skin: Produces less sebum; often feels tight, rough, or flaky.
  • Combination skin: Mix of oily and dry areas, usually with an oily T-zone (forehead, nose, chin) and normal-to-dry cheeks.
  • Sensitive skin: Reacts easily with redness, itching, or burning when exposed to certain products or triggers.





* A general, suitable routine for almost everyone 🤍:

  1. Use a gentle cleanser to wash off dirt and sebum.
  2. Use a fragrance-free moisturizer, especially in dry areas of skin.
  3. Use a sunscreen (SPF 50+) before going out in the sun.
  4. Use a lip balm on the lips.
  5. Keep hydrated!

* 💁🏽‍♂️ Haircare ~

* Discover your hair.

Just like skin, hair can have different needs. Understanding your hair type and concerns can help you create a routine that works better for you.


~ 🧴 Hair Types ~

  • Straight Hair: May benefit from lightweight products that avoid weighing the hair down
  • Wavy Hair: Can benefit from products that help maintain its natural texture and moisture.
  • Curly Hair: Often benefits from moisture-focused care and gentle handling.
  • Coily Hair: May need extra moisture and gentle care to help maintain its natural texture.

~ ✨ Haircare Basics ~

  • Cleanse your hair according to your needs
  • Use conditioner when appropriate
  • Avoid excessive heat styling
  • Avoid brushing hair when wet--unless you have curly hair
  • Keep your routine consistent

~ 🌼 Hair concerns ~

  • Dryness
  • Frizz
  • Oily scalp
  • Heat damage
