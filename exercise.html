🌼 Regular physical activity can support overall health, energy, strength, and well-being. Different forms of exercise can help people reach different goals.

~ Beginner friendly exercises 🏃🏽‍♂️~

* Strentgh building:
Helps build muscular strength and endurance.

  • Squats
  • Push-ups
  • Lunges
  • PLanks

* Cardio:
Helps improve cardiovascular endurance.

  • Walking
  • Running
  • Cycling
  • Swimming

* Flexibility & Mobility:
Helps improve movement and flexibility.

  • Stretching
  • Yoga
  • Mobility exercises

* Everyday Movement:
Exercise does not always have to mean intense workouts.

  • Walking more
  • Taking the stairs
  • Moving around regularly
  • Playing recreational sports

🎈 Find what works for you!
