* What is a healthy life without a nutritious diet?

Nutrition plays an important role in supporting energy, growth, and overall well-being. A balanced diet includes a variety of foods that provide the nutrients your body needs.



~ 🥗 Essential Nutrition ~

~ 🍙 Carbohydrates ~

  • Rice
  • Bread
  • Roti
  • Potatoes

~ 🍗 Protein ~

  • Eggs
  • Fish
  • Meat
  • Beans
  • Lentils

~ 🥑 Healthy fats ~

  • Nuts
  • Seeds
  • Avacado
  • Other fats in moderation

~ 🥕 Vitamins & Minerals ~

  • Fruits
  • Vegetables
  • Dairy
  • Legumes

~ 🥤 Hydration ~

Drinking enough fluids throughout the day helps support normal body functions. Water is generally the best choice for staying hydrated.



* 🥘 Balanced diet: A balanced diet is not about completely avoiding certain foods. It is about including a variety of foods and developing eating habits that support your lifestyle.
